LuaBridge 2.9

LuaBridge ships no licence file of its own.  Upstream states the licence and
names the copyright holders in README.md, and every source file carries an
SPDX tag rather than a notice, so the terms have to be assembled from the two.
Both are reproduced below unaltered: the README's licence section keeps its
markdown, <br> and [n] markers and all, so it can be checked against upstream
byte for byte.

Only the three section titles and the restatement of the holders are GRT's,
and the restatement adds nothing -- it is the same four notices with the link
markers taken out, because those make the names hard to read.
